Here you can find some of the projects I have worked on.

Designing Dashboard

2020

A modern and dynamic dashboard interface that enhances data visualization and user interaction.

Designed for intuitive navigation and real-time analytics, this project focused on creating a dashboard that is both visually appealing and functionally robust. It features an interactive UI, customizable widgets, and a dark/light mode for enhanced user experience.

Here are some key design elements:

The main dashboard screen, showcasing an overview of user analytics, key performance indicators, and real-time charts.

Users can customize widgets to display relevant information, allowing for a more personalized experience.

Dark mode implementation for better visibility and reduced eye strain in low-light environments.

Vibrant Portraits of 2020

2018

A creative photography project capturing diverse emotions and cultural expressions.

This photography series explores human expressions through a combination of vivid colors, high contrast, and dynamic compositions. Each portrait tells a unique story, reflecting emotions ranging from joy and serenity to intensity and mystery.

Highlighted works:

A high-contrast portrait of a smiling woman, representing the energy and positivity of urban life.

Playing with shadows and highlights, this portrait captures deep emotions through a carefully designed lighting setup.

A fusion of traditional and contemporary aesthetics, emphasizing the beauty of cultural diversity.

36 Days of Malayalam Type

2018

An artistic exploration of Malayalam typography and calligraphic styles.

This project was part of the global '36 Days of Type' challenge, where designers create unique letterforms every day. Inspired by Malayalam script, each letter was carefully crafted to reflect cultural and artistic influences while maintaining legibility.

Featured typography designs:

A highly stylized Malayalam letterform that integrates traditional motifs with modern aesthetics.

Experimenting with fluid strokes and brush techniques to enhance the organic nature of the script.

Combining digital techniques with traditional calligraphy to create a visually striking composition.
